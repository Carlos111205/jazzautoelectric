Computer & Dealer-Level Diagnostic Testing

Equipped with dealer-level diagnostics scanners and high-precision testing equipment. We perform factory-level ECU scans, sensor signal monitoring, wiring circuit analysis, and live telemetry tracking to accurately identify and resolve complex electrical issues.

Dealer-Level Diagnostics Scanners & Scopes Full Fault Code Scan & Reset Live Sensor Telemetry Diagnostics ECU Coding & Module Adaptations

Toyota Hybrid High Voltage (HV) Battery Servicing

Comprehensive battery diagnosis & replacement of all Toyota hybrids (Prius, Aqua, Axio, Fielder, Camry, Harrier, etc.). We perform high voltage battery balancing & voltage analysis, and replace weak high voltage cells to save you money over full pack replacement.

Diagnosis & Replacement of All Toyota Hybrids Battery Balancing & Individual Voltage Analysis Individual Weak High Voltage Cell Replacement Complete High Voltage Pack Refurbishment

Honda GP Dual Clutch Replacement & Servicing

All Honda GP models (Fit GP1, GP4, GP5, Vezel, Grace, Shuttle, Freed, etc.) need good care. We do full service of the dual clutch, and when failed, we replace it with a brand new clutch & release bearing along with actuator fluid servicing & calibration.

Honda GP Series Dual Clutch Replacement Brand New OEM Clutch & Release Bearing Dual Clutch Actuator Fluid Service & Bleeding Touch-Point Re-learning & Software Calibration

Electrical Repairs & Wiring Harnesses

From repairing frayed wire harnesses, splicing corroded connectors, and installing upgraded fuse boards, to resolving short circuits and fixing window regulators/door lock actuators. We repair electrical systems to factory standards.

Short Circuit Repair Wiring Harness Splicing & Re-pinning Central Locking & Power Window Repair AC Blower & Radiator Fan Electricals

Alternator & Starter Repairs

Starting problems or battery warnings are often down to the starter motor or alternator. We rebuild your starter or alternator in-house, replacing worn carbon brushes, diodes, voltage regulators, internal bearings, and solenoid relays to save you money.

Starter Solenoid Replacement Alternator Diode Plate & Brush Rebuilds Voltage Regulator Replacements Heavy Duty Truck Starter Servicing

General Auto-Electrical Troubleshooting

Whether it's a dashboard screen flickering, radio cutting out, alarms sounding randomly, or ABS/Airbag warnings illuminating, our auto-electrics engineers have the troubleshooting skills and schematics to track down the root cause.

ABS, SRS (Airbag) Fault Repair Instrument Cluster & Screen Fixes Car Alarm & Immobilizer Troubleshooting Trailer Socket & Towbar Wiring
